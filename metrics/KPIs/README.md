# VJSOS Metrics — KPIs

> Key Performance Indicators per business unit.

## Channel Metrics

### Android Channel (YouTube)
| KPI | Definition | Target | Cadence |
|-----|------------|--------|---------|
| Videos published | Count of new videos | 12/month | Weekly |
| Average retention | % watched / duration | ≥50% | Monthly |
| CTR (24h) | Click-through rate | ≥6% | Per video |
| Subscriber growth | Net new subs | ≥1K/month | Monthly |
| Revenue (ads) | Estimated RPM | $2–5 CPM | Monthly |

### Kids Studio (YouTube Kids)
| KPI | Definition | Target | Cadence |
|-----|------------|--------|---------|
| Episodes completed | Fully rendered + published | 1/week | Weekly |
| Safety gate pass rate | Child Safety approvals | 100% | Per episode |
| Average watch time | Minutes per episode | ≥2:30 | Monthly |
| Subscriber growth | Net new subs | ≥500/month | Monthly |
| Revenue (ads + licensing) | Estimated + licensing | $500/month | Quarterly |

## Production Metrics

### Pipeline Velocity
| KPI | Definition | Target | Cadence |
|-----|------------|--------|---------|
| Script → Publish time | Calendar days | ≤14 days | Per episode |
| Rework rate | % episodes needing rework | <10% | Monthly |
| Asset reuse rate | % assets reused | >60% | Quarterly |

### AI Workforce
| KPI | Definition | Target | Cadence |
|-----|------------|--------|---------|
| Agent turnaround | Time from task to output | <30s (P1–2) | Weekly |
| Escalation rate | % tasks escalated to human | <5% | Weekly |
