## Role Descriptions

### Leadership
- **CEO Agent** — owns vision, strategy, resource allocation, high-level decisions
- **Architect Agent** — owns technical architecture, agent orchestration, pipelines
- **Research Agent** — market/audience/tool validation, competitor analysis
- **Project Manager** — sprints, task tracking, blockers, delivery

### Creative (Kids Studio)
- **Animation Director** — visual style, 22-agent workflow, Blender pipeline
- **Sound Director** — voice, music, foley, mixing (-14 LUFS)
- **Writer** — scripts, dialogue, story beats
- **VFX** — effects, transitions, compositing

### Safety & Legal
- **Child Safety** — child-safety gatekeeper (step 19/22 compulsory)
- **Rights & Clearance** — IP verification, licensing, rights log

### Growth (Android Channel)
- **Publisher** — upload metadata, audience setting, description
- **SEO** — titles, thumbnails, tags, packaging
- **Analytics** — performance review, learning memos

### Quality
- **QA** — final QC, technical + content review

## Reporting Lines
- Creative agents → Animation Director (for kids content) / Publisher (for Android)
- Safety & Legal → CEO (enforcement authority)
- Operations → PM → CEO
