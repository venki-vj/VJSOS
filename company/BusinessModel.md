# VJSOS Company — Business Model

> How VJSOS creates, delivers, and captures value.

## Business Units (BU)

| Unit | Core Output | Primary Model |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| Kids Studio | "Mystery Makers Club" episodes | Ads + sponsorship + licensing |
| Android Channel | Educational tech tutorials | Ads + affiliate + courses |
| Software Products | Internal products + paid tools | Subscription / license |

## Revenue Streams

### 1. YouTube Ad Revenue
- Primary for both content units.
- Target **60%+ gross margin** on ad-generating content.
- CPM driven by retention, trust, and audience quality (not clickbait).

### 2. Sponsorships (kid-safe only)
- Vetted, non-deceptive, safe for children.
- Applied to Android Channel and Software, rarely Kids content (COPPA constraints).

### 3. Affiliate & Courses (Android Channel)
- Tool/software recommendations (transparent affiliate links).
- Paid courses for deep tutorials (Android/coding).

### 4. Content Licensing (Kids Studio)
- License episodes to EdTech platforms, schools, or streaming.
- Requires clean rights chain (see [[prompts/RIGHTS_CLEARANCE]]).

### 5. Software Products
- **Freemium:** free tools to build audience.
- **Professional:** $X/month for studio-grade features.
- **Enterprise/custom:** tailored for studios/creators.

## Unit Economics (Target)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Gross margin (content) | ≥60% |
| Gross margin (software) | ≥80% |
| CAC | <20% of LTV |
| LTV (subscription) | 3–5 years |
| Reinvestment into pipeline | ~70% |

## Cost Structure

| Cost | Type | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Software tools | Variable | Blender, DaVinci, ElevenLabs, etc. |
| Freelance voice/art | Variable | Per episode/video |
| Hardware upgrades | CapEx | Only when Phase 3 blocks revenue (see [[docs/infrastructure]]) |
| Cloud/rendering | Variable | When needed (deferred for now) |

## Capital Strategy

- **Build capability before buying capacity.**
- Reinvest ~70% of revenue into pipeline, tools, and learning.
- Keep ~30% as reserves.
- Upgrade infrastructure deliberately, phase-by-phase.

## Go-To-Market

- **Content-led:** channels + SEO + evergreen topics drive discovery.
- **Product-led:** free tools → paid upgrades.
- **Partner-led:** EdTech, creators, other studios.
