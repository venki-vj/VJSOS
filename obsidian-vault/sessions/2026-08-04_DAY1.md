## Lessons Learned

1. **Documentation-first approach works** — creating the MASTER_CONTEXT and agent prompts first gives immediate structure for all future work
2. **Phase classification is critical** — without it, we'd waste time on Phase 3-4 work the laptop can't handle
3. **Obsidian wiki links + frontmatter** creates a knowledge graph that's queryable by local LLMs
4. **SOP cadence (Daily/Weekly/Monthly)** provides immediate operational rhythm
5. **Memory persistence** ensures context survives across sessions
