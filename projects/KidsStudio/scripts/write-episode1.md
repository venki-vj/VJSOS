# Mystery Makers Club — Episode 1: The Whispering Wind Chime

## Episode Summary

The garden wind chime has lost its voice! When a paper streamer gets caught in the chime, it silences the beautiful melody. Mira, Bo, Nila, and Pip must work together — listening carefully — to free the chime and restore the garden's music.

**Lesson:** Listen before you act. Teamwork beats rushing in.

## Act I: The Visual Hook (0:00–0:25)

### Scene 1: Garden Path — Close-Up — Day
**Visual:**
- Bright blue-and-gold wind chime hangs from wooden frame
- Breeze blows — tubes swing wildly
- **Sound:** *TINK... TINK... SWISH...*
- Then silence. A long pause.
- Camera whips to PIP (cream cloud-squirrel)
- Pip's ears perk; tail pops upright into a question mark shape

### Scene 2: Garden Path — Wide Shot
**Visual:**
- MIRA, BO, NILA enter
- Mira holds a map; Nila takes notes
- Bo bounces in place like a pogo stick

**MIRA:** "The garden sound is broken! We need to find the missing note."

**BO:** "I'll find it! I'm fast! I'm bouncy!" *(squash-and-stretch leap)*

**NILA:** "Wait! We need to listen first!"

---

## Act II: The Slapstick Chase (0:40–2:00)

### Scene 3: Near the Chime Platform — Medium Shot
**Visual:**
- Bo bounds toward the chime
- Leaps to grab a tube — MISSES
- Lands on a garden watering can
- **Sound:** *BOING—CLANG!*
- Can rolls; Bo balances like a circus performer
- Camera pans rapidly

### Scene 4: Flower Beds — Wide Shot
**Visual:**
- Bo (on can) rolls toward Nila's notebook stack
- Nila gasps, cartoon-leaps away holding glasses
- Bo launches off a dirt pile ramp
- **Sound:** *ZIPPPPP!*
- Lands head-first in a hollow plastic bucket
- **Sound:** *DONKK!*

### Scene 5: Garden Pathway — Medium Shot
**Visual:**
- Bo runs blind with bucket on head
- Bumps a fence. **Sound:** *CLACK!*
- Spins dizzy; cartoon stars orbit the bucket
- Mira facepalms

**MIRA:** "Bo, freeze!"

**BO:** *(muffled)* "I found... dark! Very quiet!"

- PIP points at the chime — a PINK STREAMER caught in the gold tube

---

## Act III: The Teamwork Solution (2:00–3:00)

### Scene 6: The Chime Platform — Close-Up
**Visual:**
- Streamer flutters, blocking the gold tube from the striker

**MIRA:** "Look! A streamer is blocking the gold note!"

**BO:** *(popping bucket off)* "I'll knock it down!"

**NILA:** *(interposing)* "No more leaping! We work together. We use a tool."

- PROFESSOR PEBBLE appears holding a long mechanical grabber

**PROFESSOR PEBBLE:** "A careful team works together."

### Scene 7: Chime Platform — Wide Shot
**Visual:**
- Mira and Nila line up; Bo behind; Pip on Bo's head
- Together hold the grabber handle
- Extend toward the chime
- Close-up: claws clamp the paper streamer
- **Sound:** *CLICK-CLACK.*
- They pull together — streamer slips loose!
- Bo falls back; all four land in soft pile
- **Sound:** *WOBBLE—SLAM!*
- Camera shakes dynamically

### Scene 8: Chime Platform — High Angle
**Visual:**
- Wind blows; chime swings
- Plays: *TING... TING... SWISH... PING!*
- Complete, happy melody returns
- From the pile: Mira, Bo, Nila, Pip smile
- Pip's tail curls into a HEART shape

**MIRA:** "Slapstick solved! Team clue!"

**ALL:** "Listen, share, solve!"

**FADE OUT**

## Production Notes

### Animation Style
- Rigged 2D (Blender Grease Pencil + armature)
- Tom & Jerry-style safe slapstick
- Squash-and-stretch on all character motion

### Color Palette Reference
| Element | Hex |
|---------|-----|
| Mira jacket (teal) | #1EAAA8 |
| Bo hoodie (orange) | #F48734 |
| Nila overalls (purple) | #8963C3 |
| Pip fur (cream) | #FFF7C4 |
| Pip tail (aqua) | #60BED7 |

### Voice Direction
- **Mira:** Warm, patient, mid-range
- **Bo:** Energetic, bouncy, higher pitch
- **Nila:** Calm, rhythmic, clear
- **Pip:** Non-verbal chirps (tonal, expressive)
- **Professor:** Gentle, wise, slow

### Rendering Plan
- EEVEE for preview (fast, integrated GPU)
- Cycles for final (128 samples + denoise, run overnight)
- 1280×720 @ 30fps output
